About driving tests in Scotland

Scotland has 79 DVSA practical test centres, and 73 of them have published DVSA car figures. For April 2025 to March 2026, car pass rates across them ran from 36.4% to 73.5%, a gap of 37.1 percentage points, against 50.1% across the 311 centres we list with DVSA figures. The busiest three centres by tests conducted were Glasgow (Baillieston) (10,428 tests), Paisley (7,912 tests) and Glasgow (Shieldhall) (7,019 tests).

You can only swap with a learner booked at the same test centre, at one of its 3 nearest test centres, or at the centre you first booked at, and that rule must be met for both learners. The swap itself is made by phoning DVSA on 0300 200 1122.
