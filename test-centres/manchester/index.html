Driving test swaps in Manchester

There are 8 DVSA practical test centres for Manchester on our list: Atherton (Manchester), Bolton (Manchester), Bredbury (Manchester), Bury (Manchester), Cheetham Hill (Manchester), Rochdale (Manchester), Sale (Manchester) and West Didsbury (Manchester). In April 2025 to March 2026, car driving test pass rates across these centres ran from 44.3% at Atherton (Manchester) to 56.0% at Bredbury (Manchester), and the figure for each is shown below. You can only swap with a learner booked at the same test centre, at one of its 3 nearest test centres, or at the centre you first booked at, and that rule must be met for both learners. TestNow is a free board where you list your booked test and see who might swap with you. We do not find cancellations. The swap itself is made by phoning DVSA on 0300 200 1122.
