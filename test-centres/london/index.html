About driving tests in London

London has 30 DVSA practical test centres, and all 30 of them have published DVSA car figures. For April 2025 to March 2026, car pass rates across them ran from 39.9% to 58.7%, a gap of 18.8 percentage points, against 50.1% across the 311 centres we list with DVSA figures. The busiest three centres by tests conducted were Goodmayes (London) (21,374 tests), Morden (London) (15,770 tests) and Mitcham (London) (15,046 tests).

You can only swap with a learner booked at the same test centre, at one of its 3 nearest test centres, or at the centre you first booked at, and that rule must be met for both learners. The swap itself is made by phoning DVSA on 0300 200 1122.
