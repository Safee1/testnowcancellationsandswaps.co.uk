About driving tests in South-west England

South-west England has 22 DVSA practical test centres, and 21 of them have published DVSA car figures. For April 2025 to March 2026, car pass rates across them ran from 40.6% to 69.0%, a gap of 28.4 percentage points, against 50.1% across the 311 centres we list with DVSA figures. The busiest three centres by tests conducted were Bristol (Kingswood) (14,863 tests), Swindon (12,857 tests) and Plymouth (11,167 tests).

You can only swap with a learner booked at the same test centre, at one of its 3 nearest test centres, or at the centre you first booked at, and that rule must be met for both learners. The swap itself is made by phoning DVSA on 0300 200 1122.
