About driving tests in East Midlands

East Midlands has 20 DVSA practical test centres, and all 20 of them have published DVSA car figures. For April 2025 to March 2026, car pass rates across them ran from 40.2% to 69.7%, a gap of 29.5 percentage points, against 50.1% across the 311 centres we list with DVSA figures. The busiest three centres by tests conducted were Leicester (Wigston) (18,868 tests), Nottingham (Colwick) (15,150 tests) and Leicester (Cannock Street) (14,198 tests).

You can only swap with a learner booked at the same test centre, at one of its 3 nearest test centres, or at the centre you first booked at, and that rule must be met for both learners. The swap itself is made by phoning DVSA on 0300 200 1122.
