For driving instructors

Learners near you need an instructor for their test.

Learners on TestNow sometimes have a test booked but no instructor (or no car) for the day. When they ask for help, we can pass their details to a local instructor — that could be you.

  • Free to register — no subscription, no monthly fee.
  • Get an instant text and email the moment a learner in your regions needs an instructor.
  • Browse available learner requests in your regions (details anonymised).
  • Free while we launch — when a learner picks you, their details come straight to you by text and email. Take it or leave it; your diary stays yours.
  • Every learner has explicitly agreed to be contacted by an instructor.
  • Registered instructors get an invite to our instructors-only community group.

Save our number. Enquiry alerts are texted from 07462 169428 — save it in your phone as TestNow so you know it's a real learner and not a scam text. Enquiries are first come, first served.

We handle your data as set out in our Privacy Notice. By registering you agree that we may text and email you about learner requests in the regions you cover. If you choose to join our instructors-only community group, other members of that group will be able to see your name and mobile number. Registering doesn't commit you to anything.

We use cookies to measure how well our ads work. We never sell your data. Privacy notice.
