Frequently asked

Is this DVSA's official test availability?

No. This page shows tests that learners have listed on TestNow for a free swap, not DVSA's live booking system. Check gov.uk for official DVSA availability and bookings.

Why don't I see every test centre?

Only centres with at least one test currently listed on TestNow appear in the table. Every UK DVSA centre has its own TestNow page even with zero tests listed right now — browse them all.

How often does this update?

The figures are fetched live from TestNow's board each time you load the page, and cached in your browser for five minutes to keep things fast.
